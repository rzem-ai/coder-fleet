## What the constraint turned out to be

The script's own safety rests less on its checks than on git's refusals: git will not remove a locked worktree without a double force, and will not prune a locked entry. Once the unlock call was gone, those refusals did the rest. The leftover defect was in prose: the plan's P01, the one that defines the happy path, still said a locked worktree is removed.

## What to do differently

When the lead changes one plan decision after review, grep the plan for every place that restates it: the interface, the test list and the command decision. The reviewer should check the plan's test list against the decision as well as the code.
