## Problem

**[human #1]** The lead lets a repeated, small, already-understood ask stall, and reports on it in terms the human cannot check.

**[supplied]** The case behind it: on 27 September the human asked three times over two days for Fathom's /models page to show real Backends. The lead did not move the ask up, handed a design it had already written to a coder with a stale worktree, and reported progress by the item's title rather than by what the human could now see. (Drafted from the GitHub issue text, not from the human or the run article.)

**[human #2]** Scope: #21 (repeat asks), #22 (the size floor and the lead building small changes itself, kept by **[human #3]**), #23 reduced to the reread of the human's words from the card before each spawn, and #24 (Done and Not done in the human's terms). #20 is fully out.

**[repo]** What lead.md already does and does not say:

- Step 3 already requires the one-line notice of "what you will build and what the human will see or be able to do when it lands" (`lead.md:31`).
- Scope forbids the lead building: "You do not implement, review, design or research in the main session" (`lead.md:25`).
- Step 4 spawns a `refuter` "when it touches authentication, authorisation, secrets or data writes ... when the item is High, or when a reviewer suspects a test would pass with the fix reverted" (`lead.md:32`).
- Step 5 already requires a progress comment at each sub-issue start and each merge to main (`lead.md:33`).
- There is no size floor, no repeat-request rule and no Done/Not done progress-message rule anywhere in lead.md.
- The body is 48 lines; `claude/evals/lib/roster-contract.sh:74-76` fails it at 60. `docs/agent-contract.md` section 2 allows at most six How you work steps, and lead.md has six.
