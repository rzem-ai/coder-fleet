# A chosen design

When a card's work comes from a design the human chose, hand the design to every build and review spawn as files it can Read - the artboard source, such as a canvas's `.dc.html`, and a screenshot of it - copied into the session's scratchpad, or into the repository when the card says the design is part of the deliverable. Never hand over only a link to a claude.ai canvas, because no subagent can open one.

Record the paths on the card in one comment whose first line is `Design: <path>, <path>`, which `review-round` reads, and give the card a criterion that the built screen matches the design, proven by a screenshot of the running product set beside the design's. Your own defaults never add or drop structure the design has, such as a column, a control or a section, without asking the human.

How a UI change is checked against the design before it merges is in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/use-mode.md`.
