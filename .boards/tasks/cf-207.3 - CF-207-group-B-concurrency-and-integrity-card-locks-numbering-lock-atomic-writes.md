## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Many processes creating cards on the same board at once never receive the same number; numbers come from a board-wide lock (spec 11)
- [ ] #2 Two writers on the same card are serialised by a per-card lock file (spec 12)
- [ ] #3 A writer that finds a card's lock held waits 5 seconds and then fails with an error that names the card and the lock's holder (process and, where known, session or agent) (spec 13)
- [ ] #4 A lock whose holder's process no longer runs, or which is older than 30 seconds, is broken, and the next writer proceeds (spec 14)
- [ ] #5 A reader never sees a partly written card: a test that reads a card in a tight loop while another process rewrites it always parses a complete file, old or new (spec 15)
- [ ] #6 The card file is the source of truth: a move writes the card first and appends its event log line after; after a crash between the two, the card stands and the log lacks that line (spec 16)
<!-- AC:END -->
