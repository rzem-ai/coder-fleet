## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
What was still open on CF-193.6 after its one review round and one fix round (12 Nov 2026). Filed by the lead under its end-of-rounds rule. The human's standing decision of 12 Nov 2026, "Build with the parent", makes a Polish sub-issue part of its item's order.

1. claude/coder-fleet/workflows/review-round.js, `gatesOf` (about line 1422): it reuses `declaredGateOf`'s prefix matcher to decide whether a declared gate was reported. A green line that is the declared command narrowed to one file or test (`npx vitest run src/a.test.ts - exit 0` against a declared `npx vitest run`) counts as reporting the whole gate, so `gatesMissing` stays empty and a clean stop approves though the full gate never ran at the head. Prefix matching is right for classing a red line as a declared gate's failure; it is too loose for whether the gate ran. The round 2 reviewer's suggestion: exact match after the cd strip for `gatesOf`, prefix matching for red classification only.

2. claude/evals/lib/workflow-logic.mjs (about line 3588): `cd-prefixed-red-line-matches` covers only an unquoted cd directory. `CD_PREFIX_RE`'s quoted branches and the no-space `cd /wt&&bash` form have no case.

3. Unverified by the round 2 reviewer: `prevFix.testResults.verifiedBy` (review-round.js about line 2095), which predates CF-193.6, puts the reviewer's own gate text into the result. Whether that field ever reaches a coder brief was not traced.
