## Description

What was still open on CF-193.4 after its three review rounds (12 Nov 2026). Filed by the lead under its end-of-rounds rule; not ordered by the human, so it waits for his go.

1. claude/coder-fleet/workflows/review-round.js around line 2294: with fix: true, an approve verdict beside a red gate line and no blocking finding stops as 'clean' without commissioning a fix round. The next step opens "Not an approval: the reviewer's gate run failed" and then runs on into the clean stop's "before deciding whether to merge". Whether a red gate should start a fix round on its own is a policy the lead or the human sets.

2. `gatesFailed` holds bare line strings with no head, while `gates` entries carry the head they ran at. On a prose-only stop it can list round one's pre-fix red lines with nothing saying which commit they ran at.

3. `gatesOf` only knows the gates the reviewer chose to mention. A reviewer that reports one of four declared gates leaves `gatesMissing` empty and the round approves. The CF-193.4 coder proposed having the pin lane read the main checkout's `gates` block and name each declared gate not reported.

4. claude/evals/lib/workflow-logic.mjs around line 3310: the prose-fix table has no `.boards/` case; the board path is rejected twice over today (the not-prose directory rule and `isStateFile`), so dropping either would not turn the suite red.

Also for this card, from CF-193.5's review: the lead-procedures skill's sentence on what is never prose retypes part of the workflow's rule and leaves out fixtures, spec, e2e and __tests__ paths.
